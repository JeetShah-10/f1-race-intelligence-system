# Landing Page Vision: "The Digital Paddock"

## Core Concept
The landing page is not a marketing brochure; it is a **Mission Control Center**. It should feel like the user has hacked into the Mercedes or Red Bull strategy mainframe. The user is not a "customer"; they are a "Race Engineer".

## Section Breakdown

### 1. Act I: The Launch (Hero Section)
*   **Visual**: A high-impact "Poster" composition. A 2026 concept car cuts through giant typography.
*   **Atmosphere**: "The Garage before Q3". Dark, tense, full of potential energy. Fluid neon smoke suggests airflow.
*   **Interaction**: The user's mouse is the wind tunnel. Moving it shifts the smoke and "floating" holographic data points (Tire Deg, G-Force) around the car.
*   **Headline**: "PREDICT THE LIMIT".

### 2. Act II: The Telemetry (Features Grid)
*   **Visual**: A modular "Bento Grid" dashboard.
*   **Material**: Forged Carbon headers (representing strength) and Glass bodies (representing transparency/data).
*   **Key Modules**:
    *   **Predictive Strategy**: Large chart visualization.
    *   **Live Telemetry**: Pulse/Heartbeat line.
    *   **Secure Data Lake**: Protected "Vault" visual.

### 3. Act III: The Grid (Drivers)
*   **Visual**: "Character Selection Screen".
*   **Concept**: F1 Drivers are superheroes. We treat them as such.
*   **Interaction**: Hovering a driver "unlocks" their stats. The card lights up from grayscale to full team colors.

### 4. Act IV: The Engine (Technology)
*   **Visual**: "Neural Core" Node Graph.
*   **Concept**: Showing the brain behind the beauty. Connecting raw data points to insights.

### 5. Act V: Lights Out (CTA)
*   **Visual**: A view from the cockpit looking at the starting lights.
*   **Action**: "JOIN THE GRID". A massively high-contrast button that beckons to be pressed.

## Success Metrics (Visual)
*   **FPS**: Must maintain 60fps despite noise/blur filters.
*   **"Wow" Factor**: User must stop scrolling to play with the mouse parallax.
*   **Brand**: User must immediately associate the palette with "Premium F1" (not generic sports).
